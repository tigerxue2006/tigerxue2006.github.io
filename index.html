About Me

I am a sophomore undergraduate (Class of 2029) at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, majoring in Software Engineering at the School of Computer Science. I am currently a Research Assistant at DeepDelta Lab in the School of Artificial Intelligence, advised by Prof. Shaobo Cui.

I have a strong interest in the mathematical principles underlying deep learning models, and I hope to explore, through research, the essence of causality in models.

Current Project

I am collaborating with members of DeepDelta Lab and researchers from MemTensor to explore how causal memory can be better represented in Metis's multimodal architecture.
